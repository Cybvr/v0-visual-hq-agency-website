## PROMPT — RUN A (typography baked in)

**FORMAT**
15.0 seconds total, 9:16 vertical, 720p, real-time motion, five segments joined by four
hard cuts on the beat. Native audio on. Every important element inside the centre 80%
safe zone: nothing critical in the top 12% or bottom 20% of frame.

**REFERENCE ROLES**
@Image1 controls only the man's identity, build and wardrobe. Do not copy its pose,
backdrop, grid layout, cream studio sweep, captions or camera angle.
@Image2 controls only his facial structure and skin. Do not copy its lighting or framing.
@Image3 controls only the architecture, surfaces and props of the two room states. Do not
copy its people, framing, panel layout or on-image titles.

**GLOBAL STYLE**
Modern performance-ad look, not a film-look drama. Photoreal, high-contrast, crisp digital
capture with a shallow but controlled depth of field. Colour grade: cool desaturated slate
and grey-green in segments 1 and 2, snapping to warm neutral bone-white with a single
oxblood-red accent from segment 3 onward. Palette limited to bone white #F2EDE4, near-black
#111111, warm mid-grey, and one accent red #8E1128 — no other saturated colour anywhere.
Shutter behaviour slightly sharp, 1/100-ish, so fast motion strobes a little and reads as
energetic rather than smeared. NOT cinematic-soft, NOT teal-and-orange, NOT a CGI render,
NOT slow motion, NOT drone footage.

**CHARACTER**
One man, alone, mid-thirties, West African, warm deep-brown skin with visible natural
texture: fine pores across the nose and cheeks, a slight sheen of sweat on the forehead and
above the upper lip in the first two segments that reads as real skin and not oil paint,
faint specular highlight on the brow ridge, subtle skin reddening at the inner eye corner.
Short cropped natural hair with a clean tapered fade at the temples, softly defined
hairline. Full but neatly trimmed beard with a moustache joined to the beard, a few grey
hairs at the chin. Straight nose, broad cheekbones, heavy-lidded dark brown eyes, direct
unblinking gaze when he addresses camera. Medium-athletic build, broad shoulders, roughly
6ft. No glasses, no jewellery, no watch, no tattoos.

**WARDROBE**
Charcoal-black brushed-cotton overshirt, button front, two chest pockets, worn open and
unbuttoned, cuffs rolled once to mid-forearm, visible weave and a soft matte finish that
eats light rather than shining. Underneath, a cream off-white heavyweight crewneck
t-shirt with a slightly ribbed collar and a soft brushed nap. Black tapered trousers, flat
front, no break. Black leather derby shoes, low sheen. Wardrobe is identical in every
segment — no costume change, no jacket removal, no tie, no suit.

**LOCATION**
Segments 1–2, THE BROKEN ROOM: a cramped back office in a Lagos house. Bare grey concrete
walls with patchy skim and damp shadow, a louvred steel-barred window with dusty daylight
raking through the slats, a heavy dark-wood desk with a scratched top, a black anglepoise
desk lamp, a swivel chair, terracotta floor tiles, curling paper notices pinned to the
wall. Cluttered, airless, dust visible in the light shafts.
Segments 3–5, THE BUILT ROOM: the same footprint reorganised into a controlled bone-white
studio workspace. Clean plaster walls, timber-framed windows behind sheer linen curtains
lifting slightly, a long pale ash worktable, a single matte-black monitor angled away from
camera, one green plant, one bone-white ceramic mug, terracotta tiles now swept and warm.
Same room geometry, same window positions, same door position — only the state changes.

**PROPS**
Three identical matte-black smartphones with cracked-free screens showing dense stacked
notification banners in white on black. One mountain of loose A4 paper — invoices,
spreadsheets, handwritten notes, a stapled contract, edges dog-eared and slightly curled.
One black desk lamp. Nothing else on the desk in segment 1.

**FIRST FRAME AND BLOCKING**
Extreme close-up, camera at desk height, lens level with the desk surface, the man's
torso filling the right two-thirds of frame and racked slightly soft. The empty dark desk
top runs across the bottom third in sharp focus. Room dim, one hard slatted daylight bar
across the back wall behind him. Nothing is moving. No text on screen.

### SEGMENT 1 — 0.0s to 2.0s — PATTERN INTERRUPT
Three phones slam down onto the desk one after another, at 0.2s, 0.5s and 0.8s, each one
entering from a different edge of frame and landing hard enough to make the desk surface
jump. Notification banners stack and multiply across all three screens on each impact. At
1.0s the man drops into the frame from above, plants both palms flat on the desk either
side of the phones, leans in until his face fills the upper half of the frame, and looks
directly down the lens. He speaks one line. He does not blink until the cut.
CAMERA: locked-off macro at desk height for the three slams, then a single fast 25cm
push-in as he leans in. One move only, starting at 1.0s and stopping dead at 1.6s.
TYPE: at 1.2s, in the dark negative space behind his shoulder, the line
STOP USING PEOPLE LIKE SOFTWARE snaps on in three fast stacked steps, one word group per
frame beat, no fade, no animation drift.
HARD CUT at 2.0s.

### SEGMENT 2 — 2.0s to 6.0s — ESCALATION
He drags the paper mountain across the desk with both forearms, gathers it, and shoves the
whole stack directly into the lens until loose sheets blur past camera and one sheet slaps
flat against the front element and slides off. On the impact beat at 3.6s the paper
dissolves into three clean flat interface cards that assemble in the air in front of him,
bone-white cards with thin rules and a single red status dot, arriving one per beat at
3.6s, 4.4s and 5.2s, each one settling into a locked position and holding still.
CAMERA: handheld, tight, chest-height, drifting 10cm left and correcting, reacting to the
shove with a small shake at 3.6s and settling. No zoom, no dolly.
TYPE: each card carries one line of its own, appearing with the card —
COPYING → AUTOMATED, then CHASING → RESOLVED, then UPDATING → DONE.
HARD CUT at 6.0s.

### SEGMENT 3 — 6.0s to 10.0s — PRODUCT REVEAL
He sweeps his right hand flat across the frame from left to right at 6.1s. His hand wipes
the cluttered dim room out of frame and reveals the bone-white built room behind it in a
single continuous whip, the transition completing at 6.5s. Grade snaps warm on the same
frame. He lowers his hand, straightens, and turns slowly on the spot to face camera square
by 7.4s, then holds, breathing, calm, shoulders dropped. Dust in the air is gone. Curtains
lift once behind him.
CAMERA: one continuous whip pan left to right locked to his hand, decelerating into a
static medium-wide as he settles. The camera stops moving at 7.4s and does not move again
in this segment.
TYPE: three labels lock onto objects around him and track with the frame —
AGENTIC SYSTEMS anchored to the monitor, INTERNAL APPS anchored to the worktable,
DIGITAL PRODUCTS anchored to the window, arriving at 7.6s, 8.3s and 9.0s, each with a thin
red leader line to the object it names.
HARD CUT at 10.0s.

### SEGMENT 4 — 10.0s to 13.0s — EXPAND
He walks steadily toward camera down the length of the room, arms loose, eyes on the lens,
covering about three metres. Behind him the wall content changes three times without a
cut, each change landing on his footfall: at 10.6s a strategy wall of pinned diagrams and
index cards, at 11.4s a lit campaign photo set with a stand light and a seamless backdrop,
at 12.2s a wall of vertical creator-content screens playing silently. Room geometry stays
identical through all three; only the wall dressing changes.
CAMERA: slow steady dolly-back at chest height, retreating at exactly his walking pace so
his framing stays constant from waist-up. One behaviour, continuous, no speed change.
TYPE: three lines follow his movement, tracking with him and holding to his left —
BUILD IT. at 10.6s, POSITION IT. at 11.4s, PUT IT IN FRONT OF PEOPLE. at 12.2s.
HARD CUT at 13.0s.

### SEGMENT 5 — 13.0s to 15.0s — HARD CLOSE
He takes one last step and stops inches from the lens, face filling the frame, eyes dead
centre, holding still. At 13.6s everything cuts to solid black.
CAMERA: static. No move at all in this segment.
TYPE: on black, four beats, one line snapping on per beat in bone white, centred, each
replacing the last — BUILD THE SYSTEM. at 13.7s, SHAPE THE BRAND. at 14.0s,
MOVE THE MARKET. at 14.3s. At 14.6s all three clear and the wordmark VisualCNS appears
centred in bone white with a thin oxblood rule beneath it, and directly under it the
smaller line DM "BUILD". Both hold, motionless, to 15.0s.

**OPTICS**
Segment 1: 35mm equivalent, close macro, camera 40cm from his face, deep enough focus to
hold both the phones and his eyes.
Segment 2: 28mm equivalent, wide and close, exaggerated foreground so the paper reads huge
against the lens.
Segment 3: 40mm equivalent, camera at chest height, full body in frame with headroom.
Segment 4: 50mm equivalent, chest height, compressed so the changing back wall reads flat
and graphic.
Segment 5: 35mm equivalent, camera 25cm from his face, focus locked on his near eye.
No lens flares, no anamorphic streaks, no fisheye distortion, no rack-focus hunting.

**LIGHTING**
Segments 1–2: single hard motivated source from the louvred window camera-left, cutting
slatted bars across the back wall and one bar across his cheek. Deep unlifted shadows,
crushed blacks, a weak cold bounce from the phone screens under his chin. Practical desk
lamp glows in frame but does not light his face. Contrast high, mood airless.
Segments 3–5: soft broad daylight through sheer curtains camera-right wrapping his face,
a clean fill from the bone-white wall opposite, shadows open and warm, skin retains its
texture and specular highlights without going flat or beauty-lit. Same key direction held
across all three segments. Segment 5 keeps the same key but falls off fast to black
behind him.

**PHYSICS**
Paper behaves like real paper: sheets fan, buckle, catch air, flutter at different rates
and fall out of frame — no rigid slabs, no identical copies. Phones land with a real
weight and a tiny rebound. Fabric of the overshirt swings and settles on his movement,
never stiff. Dust motes drift in the light shafts in segments 1–2 only. Curtains lift and
fall once in segment 3. His hands stay whole and five-fingered at all times, fingers never
merge with the desk or the paper.

**TYPOGRAPHY**
All on-screen text is a single family: a heavy geometric sans, uppercase, tight letter
spacing, bone white #F2EDE4 on dark and near-black #111111 on light, with the accent red
#8E1128 used only for the arrows, the status dots, the leader lines and the rule under the
wordmark. Type is flat 2D graphic overlay, always perfectly sharp, always spelled exactly
as written, never handwritten, never 3D, never bevelled, never warped to the surface,
never drifting or floating. Every line snaps on in one frame with no fade or blur. Lines
sit inside the centre safe area. The only readable text anywhere in the video is the lines
listed in the segments above — every other surface, notification banner, paper document,
sign and screen in frame is illegible.

**AUDIO**
Dialogue, spoken by the man directly to camera, one line only, 1.2s to 1.9s:
"Your team is not an API." Delivered flat, low, certain, Nigerian-accented English, no
smile, no rise at the end.
Voiceover, same male voice, off-camera, calm and level, mixed above the bed:
3.0s to 5.8s — "If they spend all day copying, chasing and updating, you don't need more staff."
7.0s to 9.6s — "You need systems built around how your business actually works."
10.4s to 12.8s — "Then strategy, creative and influence that make the market care."
SFX: three hard desk impacts at 0.2s, 0.5s and 0.8s; layered notification pings stacking
on each impact; a heavy paper rush and slap at 3.4s to 3.7s; three soft interface clicks
at 3.6s, 4.4s and 5.2s; a low whoosh on the hand-wipe at 6.1s to 6.5s; three quiet locking
ticks at 7.6s, 8.3s and 9.0s; footsteps on tile through segment 4; a single deep sub-drop
at 13.6s on the cut to black; three sharp type-snap ticks at 13.7s, 14.0s and 14.3s.
AMBIENCE: close airless room tone with a faint ceiling-fan hum in segments 1–2, opening
into quiet clean air with distant outdoor Lagos traffic in segments 3–5.
MUSIC: one driving minimal percussive bed at roughly 110bpm, kick landing on every cut,
dropping out completely for 0.3s at 13.6s before the final ticks.
No subtitles, no captions, no crowd noise, no second voice, no laughter.

**CONTINUITY LOCKS**
Face lock: identical facial structure, hairline, beard shape and skin texture in every
segment, zero drift, no age change, no weight change. Wardrobe lock: same overshirt, same
cream tee, same trousers, same shoes, same rolled cuffs throughout — the shirt stays open
and unbuttoned in all five segments. He is the only person in the entire video. Room
geometry lock: the window is camera-left in segments 1–2 and camera-right in segments 3–5
because the camera has moved around, not because the room changed; wall positions, door
position and floor tiles are the same building throughout. Direction lock: his motion
carries left-to-right across the cuts and never reverses. State lock: clutter only ever
decreases, order only ever increases, never resets.

**ENDING STATE**
Final frame is solid black. Centred, motionless, sharp: the wordmark VisualCNS in bone
white, a thin oxblood-red rule beneath it, and under that the smaller line DM "BUILD".
No man, no room, no motion, no residual light.

**CONSTRAINTS**
No cuts other than the four specified hard cuts. No slow motion, no speed ramps, no
freeze frames. No morphing faces, no eye drift, no warped or extra fingers, no merged
hands. No second person, no crowd, no children. No camera shake beyond the specified
handheld in segment 2. No text other than the listed lines. No logos other than the
VisualCNS wordmark in the final two seconds. No brand names on the phones or monitor. No
readable text on any paper, screen or notification. No subtitles or auto-captions. No
watermark. No stock-footage business imagery, no handshakes, no boardrooms, no city
skylines, no rising graphs. No lens flare, no light leaks, no film grain overlay, no
letterboxing, no split screens, no vignette. No repeated actions, no duplicated props,
no props appearing that were not named.

## RUN B — clean plates (recommended for the real deliverable)

Same prompt, with two swaps:

1. Delete every `TYPE:` line and the `TYPOGRAPHY` section. Replace with:
   *"No on-screen text or graphics of any kind. All signage, paper, notifications and
   screens are illegible. Leave the specified negative space empty and evenly lit so
   graphics can be composited later."*
2. In `AUDIO`, keep only the 1.2s–1.9s sync line and the SFX/ambience; delete the
   voiceover blocks and record VO separately.

Then composite the supers, the wordmark and the VO in After Effects or CapCut. Reason: the
supers are the whole idea here, and Seedance still misspells and warps multi-line type
often enough that a 15-line kinetic sequence will not survive a single generation. The
negative-space blocking above is already written so the plates receive type cleanly.

## Practical notes

- Generate in 9:16 at 720p, duration 15, native audio on.
- Attach one reference per invariant only — the three listed. Do not add more.
- If a generation misses, change one layer at a time: references and identity first, then
  segment structure, then camera, then style and audio.
- The high-risk segment is 3 (the hand-wipe room transformation). If it fails, split the
  job: generate segments 1–2 and 3–5 as two clips and cut them together on the 6.0s beat.
- Accent red is taken from the continuity sheets' rule colour; swap `#8E1128` if the
  VisualCNS brand red differs.
